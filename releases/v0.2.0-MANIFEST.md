## Explicit non-claims

Version 0.2.0 does not claim:

- arbitrary natural-language understanding in O(1);
- invention of aliases, symbolic IDs, graph traversal, databases or content addressing;
- invention of policy-first routing, load balancing, access control or heterogeneous scheduling;
- invention of event time, semi-Markov processes, entropy rate, combinatorial ranking or variable-length coding;
- arbitrary lossless compression beyond information-theoretic bounds;
- recovery of arbitrary information from a fixed-size hash alone;
- universal superiority of Resolutive mechanisms over established systems.
